Why Nestly

Repairs shouldn't depend on who you know

Most hostel problems aren't hard to fix. They're hard to track. Nestly replaces scattered messages and word of mouth with one clear record everyone can trust.

Without a system

  • Complaints told verbally, then forgotten
  • No idea who is handling the problem
  • Repeated follow-ups just to get an update
  • Management can't see what is delayed

With Nestly

  • Every complaint logged with room and category
  • A named technician assigned and visible to you
  • Progress updates without having to ask
  • Clear reports for wardens and administrators

Status lifecycle

Know exactly where your complaint stands

Every complaint moves through clear stages, and each change is recorded with the time it happened. No guessing, no chasing.

  • Submitted

    Your complaint is received and logged with a reference number.

  • Under review

    The warden confirms the details and sets the priority.

  • Assigned

    A technician is chosen and the job appears on their list.

  • In progress

    Work is underway, with notes added as it moves forward.

  • Resolved

    The repair is done. You confirm it and leave a rating.

Questions

Things people often ask

Can't find your answer? Sign in and speak to your hall warden.

Who can use Nestly?

Students, hall wardens, maintenance technicians and university administrators. Each role signs in to its own portal.

How do I know my complaint was received?

As soon as you submit, it appears on your dashboard with a reference number and a "Submitted" status.

Can I see who is fixing the problem?

Yes. Once the warden assigns a technician, the assignment is shown on your complaint along with progress updates.

What if it isn't properly fixed?

You confirm the repair when it's done. If something is still wrong, you can say so and the complaint can be revisited.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The portal adapts to phones, tablets and laptops, so you can report from your room.
